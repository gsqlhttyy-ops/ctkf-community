# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, technically honest, and constructive. Welcome people with
different backgrounds and experience levels. Critique ideas and evidence, not
people. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, expose private information, or
misrepresent simulated evidence as real production proof.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, reject, remove, or lock contributions and
discussions that violate this standard. Report conduct concerns privately to the
repository owner. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and without retaliation.
